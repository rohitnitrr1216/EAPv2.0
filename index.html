Dam Inundation – Submerged State & District Finder

For Emergency Action Plan (EAP) preparation

  1. Select the type of data in your TIFF (depth, velocity, etc.).
  2. Click the box below and choose your inundation map (GeoTIFF).
  3. Wait for the result table to appear.
  4. Download the table (CSV) and the map (PDF) and use them in the EAP.
